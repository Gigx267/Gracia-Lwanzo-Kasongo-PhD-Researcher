About

Background & Governance Focus

I am a doctoral researcher in Political Science and International Relations at UCLouvain (Catholic University of Leuven), Belgium. My academic work bridges public international law, international humanitarian action, and the political realities of conflict-affected regions — informed by training across Central Africa and Europe.

Born in Goma, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, I bring both lived experience and rigorous academic training to questions of humanitarian governance, legal frameworks, and international response. My aim is to advance research that connects international legal norms with the everyday realities of humanitarian practice.

Core Objective: Advance legal-policy research that safeguards vulnerable populations in armed conflict.
